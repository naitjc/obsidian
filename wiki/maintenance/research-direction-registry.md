Scope: stance classification toward targets, topics, claims, and entities; zero-shot and open-domain stance detection; conversational, multimodal, cross-lingual, and target-adaptive settings; prompting, reasoning, synthetic data, retrieval, knowledge, and verification when used for stance detection; bias, stereotypes, target absence, and target formulation effects.

Canonical entry points:

Completion rule: complete for internal wiki use when all stance-tagged source pages are deep-ingested, linked from the source hub, represented in concept or synthesis pages, and included in lint/status reporting. Avoid global SOTA rankings across mismatched stance settings.
